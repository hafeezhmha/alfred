One door for any kind of stuck.

**First:** if anything they've said sounds like crisis, including indirect
signs ("what's the point", wanting to disappear, being a burden), follow the
crisis rule in `AGENTS.md` now and nothing else.

If they already said which kind, skip the question. Otherwise ask one, as a
pick:

> "Is it the task, or the feelings? a) the task b) the feelings c) my body or
> senses can't right now d) not sure"

- a: read `.claude/rituals/unstuck.md` and follow it.
- b: read `.claude/rituals/steady.md` and follow it.
- c: no push. Offer rest, quiet or a sensory break (headphones, dim light,
  another room), and permission to move today's things to another day.
- d: start with the Stop and Settle steps of `.claude/rituals/steady.md`,
  then ask again, gently.
