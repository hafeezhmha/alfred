# Topic guidance

One section per topic in `to-learn.md`. `./life learn` prints only the
section being asked. Every question: one at a time, a suggested answer or a
pick, "skip" is a full answer.

## Energy

"When is your brain sharpest, and when does it crash?" Offer a pick: mornings
/ late morning / afternoon / night / it changes. Write the pattern to
`context.md` "How my brain works". If it points to a planning time, offer:
"Want planning to happen around then?" and write `.life/midpoint` (HH:MM, the
point after which `/life` wraps up instead of planning).

## Day shape

Office or home days, commute length, calls outside normal hours (US or Europe
teams). Don't assume mornings; the day may start at 11:00 after a late call.
Write to `context.md` "Fixed commitments" and "Constraints".

## Home

Alone, flatmates or PG, parents or joint family, partner or in-laws. This
decides quiet time, shared devices, and family expectations (festivals,
weddings, visits, caring for parents). Ask "OK to keep this in the repo, or
private?" If others see their screen, note it in "About the person"
("screen seen by others") and offer `./life nudge on HH:MM private`.

## People

Who should the agent know about, and one thing to remember for each ("call
Sundays", "prefers a message"). Ask "OK to keep this in the repo, or
private?" Write to `context.md` "People who matter", or `private/support.md`.

## Fixed commitments

What happens at set times every week: work hours, classes, caregiving,
family time. Family time is a commitment, not a distraction. `context.md`
"Fixed commitments".

## Language

"English, or a mix like you'd text a friend? a) English b) English with
Hindi / Kannada / Tamil / your language mixed in c) mostly your language."
Mirror it in replies; files stay in English. "About the person".

## Areas

Which 3 or 4 parts of life matter most this season (ideas: health, money,
work, study, family, admin, home, friends, creative). For each: a row in
`context.md` "Areas" and `areas/<slug>/README.md` from `areas/_template.md`.
Fewer is fine.

## Capture

Where thoughts land today: WhatsApp "message yourself", notes app, paper,
head. Where their eyes already go wins. WhatsApp or notes: wrap-up will ask
once for new lines to paste. `context.md` "Tools".

## Brain

"Anything about how your brain works you'd like me to know? Diagnosed,
self-identified or just wondering all count the same." Optional, never
probed. Never ask whether it's diagnosed; never write "diagnosed" unless they
said it. Ask "OK to keep this in the repo, or private?" If ADHD-like: offer
ADHD mode. Autistic or sensory-sensitive: ask what routines they rely on;
predictability then outranks novelty.

## Past systems

What they've tried and what made each stop (too much upkeep, novelty wore
off, one bad week and shame did the rest, an app they never opened). Name
the pattern back without judgment, and write the pattern (not the story) to
`context.md` "How my brain works".

## Nudge

"A daily reminder on your computer, only on days you haven't opened this?"
Skip it if they learn to ignore reminders. On a yes, say it changes their
computer's scheduler, then `./life nudge on HH:MM` (add `private` if others
see their screen) and `./life nudge test`.

## Support

"Do you see a therapist or counsellor? What kind, and anything they want you
practising?" Details go ONLY to `private/support.md`; "About the person"
says just "Support: see private/support.md". Their skills come first in
`/stuck`. No therapist and want options: Tele-MANAS 14416 (free), NIMHANS,
college and NGO clinics with lower fees.

## Parts

"Do you like talking in parts, like 'a part of me doesn't want to'? a) yes
b) sometimes c) no, sounds odd." Write "Parts language: ..." in "About the
person". If they do IFS or know their parts: "Want me to know the parts you
work with, by your names for them?" Protectors only, under "Parts I know" in
`private/support.md`. A young or hurt part: one line under "For my therapist
only, never addressed", and never engaged, even by name.

## Never suggest

"Anything I should never bring up or suggest?" (e.g. "no 5am routines", "no
calorie tracking"). "About the person" under "Never suggest".
