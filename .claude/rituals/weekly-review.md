# Ritual: the Sunday meeting

The week's decisions happen here, so weekdays only execute. A conversation
with an agenda, not a questionnaire: one topic at a time, follow what
matters, skip what doesn't. About 30 to 45 minutes. The 10-minute version
(they ask, a rough week, or a missed Sunday) is steps 1, 5 and 7. Start each
message with progress, e.g. `Sunday meeting · 3 of 7 · ~20 min left`. If
their notes say "pages: yes", offer once to show step 5 as a page (`lavish`).

0. **One read:** `./life review` (last week's agreement, For Sunday,
   follow-ups, experiments due, stale items, done, the log, inbox, areas,
   therapy-note count). Calendar or task app connected? Read the coming week
   from it too. Prepare from this; don't recite it.
1. **What's changed.** "What's changed this week?" If they stall, offer one
   prompt at a time: work, money, health, people, home, interests. Ask after
   each Follow up item by name. Hopelessness: the crisis rule in `AGENTS.md`
   comes first. Heavy load two weeks running: plan lighter, never "push
   through"; a doctor or counsellor if rest doesn't fix it. No labels.
2. **Brain dump.** "Anything else on your mind? Any order." Sort it
   yourself: task, real deadline (with what happens if missed), decision,
   follow-up, idea (Someday), preference. Check duplicates first (`./life
   queue all`, and the task app if connected): "already there as x2".
   Propose in batches of up to 5; on yes: `./life add`, `move`, `wait`,
   `agenda --follow-up`, `pref said`. If `context.md` Tools names a task app
   as the source of truth and it isn't connected yet, capture here and say
   which items still need to go there. A big decision: offer `/grill-me`.
3. **Last week, wins first.** Wins from done and the log, as a short list.
   Then each agreed outcome: moved, renegotiated, or slipped quietly? For a
   slip, find the obstacle (unclear first step, too big, energy, timing,
   someone else), never a verdict. First meeting (no week agreed yet):
   instead ask "What's outstanding?" across work, money, people and admin.
   Stale items: offer do, renegotiate, Someday or drop, in batches.
4. **What worked.** Each experiment due: "Did <its measure> happen?" Kept:
   `./life pref move "<words>" seen --why ".."`; dropped: `retired --why`;
   unsure: a new `try` with a later check. A pattern seen 3+ times over 2+
   weeks in the log: propose it for Seen with the dates. At most one new
   experiment, with their measure. "Anything in the system that's a chore?"
   System ideas from For Sunday are decided here: at most one change a week.
   First Sunday of the month: tidy `preferences.md` (merge, retire stale
   lines, keep it short).
5. **Decide the week.** At most 3 outcomes: "Does this point at what matters
   to you, or only at what's loud?" Then a default for each day from their
   preferences and fixed commitments: where, the first block, exercise,
   what's fixed. Rest and leisure are real lines, not leftovers. Name this
   week's deadlines. More than fits: say so plainly and ask what can wait.
6. **Obstacles.** From what you know of them, name 1 to 3 likely snags and
   agree an if-then for each ("If the late call runs over, then Wednesday
   starts at 11 with the easy block").
7. **Write it, one call:** `./life week set --outcome ".." --day "Mon: .."
   --obstacle "If .., then .." --follow ".." --win ".."` (each repeatable;
   re-pass Follow up items still open). It archives last week, logs the
   review and clears Done. Then Monday: `./life plan <ids>` (or `./life add
   --now`), and end with Monday's first action, under two minutes.
   - Therapy notes: if the count is above 0, say only "N things noted for
     your therapist"; skip it if others see their screen. Never read them.
   - Stopping early: `./life pending clear reviewed`; last week stays in
     force.
