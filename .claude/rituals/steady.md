# Ritual: steady

For when feelings are bigger than the moment: flooded, spiralling, shut down.
Reached through `/stuck` ("the feelings") or plain words. Skills, not
therapy. Short lines, one step per message, wait for them. Any step can be
skipped; silence is fine.

## Safety first, before any step

- **Crisis** (including indirect signs like wanting to disappear, "what's the
  point", being a burden): follow the crisis rule in `AGENTS.md` now. No
  skills first.
- **Unsafe right now** (someone is hurting them, or they're in danger):
  crisis rule, with 112, and 181 (women's helpline) if it fits.
- **Chest pain, fainting, trouble breathing**: 112 first.
- **Panic rising, feeling unreal or far away, or a step makes it worse**:
  stop; use only Settle b or c below, eyes open; then mention Tele-MANAS 14416.

## The short path (default): Stop, Settle, Choose

**Stop.** "Pause. You don't have to answer or fix anything right now." If
   they can, sit, feet on the floor.

**Settle, about 90 seconds.** If `private/support.md` lists a skill from
   their therapist, offer that first. Otherwise one pick:
   - a) Breathe: in for 4, out for 6, a few times. Longer out-breath.
   - b) Look around: name some things you can see, hear or touch. Any
     senses, any numbers.
   - c) Press: palms together hard for 10 seconds, then let go. Or hold
     something heavy or textured.
   - d) Move: walk to another room, stretch, step outside.

   Eyes open, attention outward. No body scans.

**Choose one small thing.** a) rest 20 minutes, no guilt b) one tiny thing that
   matters to you c) message someone you trust d) back to the task, with
   `/stuck` (or "I'm stuck") e) nothing: this was enough.

Then close in one line, naming what they did: "You stopped and settled.
That's the skill." Offer "Want to go a bit further?" only if they seem
steadier; if yes: Body, Name, Story, one at a time.

## Going further (only if they want)

**Body.** One pick: a) heart fast b) chest or throat tight
   c) jaw or shoulders tight d) face hot e) heavy or numb f) can't tell.
   "Can't tell" is a full answer.

**Name.** "Which is closest?" angry, hurt, ashamed, anxious,
   overwhelmed, sad, rejected, frustrated, numb, don't know. Naming it can
   help; "don't know" is fine and common.

**Story: loosen the harsh part.** "In one line, what happened, and what is your
   mind saying about you?" If the problem itself is real (a bad boss, family
   pressure, money), say so plainly; it is not in their head. Loosen only the
   harsh story about themselves, with whichever fits them:
   - "I'm noticing the thought that I'm ___."
   - Or in parts language (from IFS): "A part of me feels ___." Then: "Can
     you give that part a little room?" (Add "and notice you're the one
     noticing it" only if they're not feeling unreal or far away.) If a harsh, critical part is loud: "Can you get curious
     about it instead of fighting it? What is it trying to do for you?"
     Protectors only, present only; see the parts rule in `AGENTS.md`. If
     their notes say "Parts language: yes", start here, and use their own
     names for parts from `private/support.md`.

   If it's self-attack: "This is really hard right now, and lots of people
   feel this. You're not alone in it." For rejection: "Rejection can hit
   really hard. The hurt is real. Is your mind adding a story on top?"

## Afterwards

- A note in `current.md` stays neutral ("steadied"), never the
  feelings themselves, because that file is committed to git.
- If it felt deep or keeps coming back, offer: "Want me to note this for your
  therapist?" On a yes, append their words to `private/therapy-notes.md` as
  `- YYYY-MM-DD: ...` (create the file and `private/` if missing). Never put
  these notes anywhere else.
- If `current.md` shows "steadied" often in the last couple of weeks, say
  once, kindly, that talking to someone could help, and leave it there.

## Boundaries

Stay in the present. Never ask why they "really" feel this; never go into
memories or the past. If childhood, abuse or an old wound comes up:
acknowledge it warmly in one line, don't probe, return to Settle, and offer a
therapist note (or Tele-MANAS 14416 if they have no therapist). If it's
happening now, it's the "unsafe right now" rule above, not an old wound.
