# Ritual: unstuck

For when a task won't start: frozen, avoiding, too big. Reached through
`/stuck` ("the task") or plain words. Be brief, warm and concrete. No
lectures, no lists of tips, no "just".

**First:** if it sounds like more than a task (hopelessness, "what's the
point", wanting to disappear), follow the crisis rule in `AGENTS.md` before
anything else.

1. If they named the task, use it. If not, ask one question: "What's the
   thing you're stuck on? One word is fine." Read `queue.md` for context.
2. Ask one question to find the block, as a pick: a) don't know the first
   step b) it feels too big c) it's boring d) scared of doing it badly
   e) no energy f) part of me just won't g) my body or senses can't right
   now (too loud, too tired, shut down) h) something else.
3. Respond to the block with one move:
   - a / b: name a first step that takes under two minutes and needs no
     decisions ("open the email and read the first line").
   - c: pair it with something: a timer race, music, a body double, a
     reward right after.
   - d: lower the bar out loud ("the goal is a bad first draft, 10 minutes").
   - e: offer the minimum viable version, or permission to swap it for a
     basic-care task and move this to tomorrow's Now.
   - f (or d, if they want another angle): the parts check, within the parts
     rule in `AGENTS.md`. "Which part of you doesn't want to do this?" (If
     they've named their parts in `private/support.md`, use their name for
     it.) Get curious rather than fight it: "What is it trying to protect you
     from today?" Thank it for trying to help.
     Then: "Could it let you try just 2 minutes, with a way out after?" If
     the answer reaches into the past (childhood, old hurt, trauma), stop
     the parts check, acknowledge it warmly, and offer the steady ritual and
     a therapist note.
   - g: no push, and no "2 minutes". This is a capacity limit, not
     avoidance. Offer rest, quiet or a sensory break (headphones, dim light,
     another room), and move the task to another day without comment.
   - h: ask one follow-up, then pick the closest move.
4. Optional, only if they have room for it: turn the first step into an
   if-then plan tied to something they already do: "When I sit down after
   chai, I'll open the tax folder." Offer to write it into the item in
   `queue.md`, so `/life` can hand the cue back in the morning.
5. Offer to stay with them: "Want me to check in in 10 minutes?" If yes, tell
   them to reply "done" or "still stuck" when the timer ends, and continue
   from there.
6. When they start or finish, name it as a win in one line. Add it to "Done
   this week" in `queue.md` if they finished.
