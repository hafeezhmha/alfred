# Preferences

How this person works, kept by evidence so plans fit them better over time.
One dated line each. Said, Seen and Trying shape plans; Guessing never does,
it only suggests experiments. Written with `./life pref`; tidied monthly at
the Sunday meeting. Health and family details go in `private/`, not here.

## Said

What they told you directly, in their words.

## Seen

A pattern seen at least 3 times across at least 2 weeks, confirmed by them.

## Guessing

A possible pattern or reason. One day is never enough to move it up.

## Trying

Experiments, at most 2 at once, each with a check date and what "it helped"
means.

## Retired

Stopped, with why. Not suggested again unless their life changes.
