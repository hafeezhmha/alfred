# Example: Kavya

A made-up person, to show what Life OS looks like after a few weeks of use.
Kavya is 27, a backend developer in Bangalore, lives with her parents, and
thinks she has ADHD but hasn't been assessed. Her sister gets married in
December.

What's here: `context.md`, `current.md`, `queue.md`, one area
(`areas/family/`), `about-the-person.md` (the section setup writes into
`AGENTS.md`), and `support.md` (in a real copy this is
`private/support.md`, which git ignores).

What setup picked up (three questions on day one, the rest one at a time), and
what it designed because of it:

- **Every planner she tried died after one bad week, because the backlog felt
  like judgment.** So there's a fresh start every day, no overdue list, and
  Now shows at most 2 things.
- **She focuses best 11:00 to 14:00 on WFH days, and office days are gone to
  the commute.** So `/life` plans the day at 11:00 on WFH days, and office days
  get one small Now item.
- **Late US calls twice a week.** So the morning after a call starts slow, and
  that's planned, not a failure.
- **Thoughts land in WhatsApp "message yourself".** So that's her capture
  tool, swept into the Inbox at wrap-up.
- **Lives with family; her laptop is sometimes seen.** So the nudge is in
  private mode: it never names the task on screen.
- **The wedding could eat every weekend.** She settled it with `/grill-me`:
  she owns three wedding jobs, not everything.
